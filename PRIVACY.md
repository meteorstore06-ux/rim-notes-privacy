# Privacy Policy — Rim Notes

**Effective date:** 15 November 2026
**Applies to:** the Rim Notes Windows desktop application (all versions), distributed as paid software.

---

## Plain-language summary

**Rim Notes is 100% offline. It collects zero personal data, has no telemetry, and uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking. Everything you write stays on your own computer.**

---

## 1. What data the app stores

Rim Notes stores the following **locally on your device only**, in your own user profile folder:

```
%AppData%\RimNotes\
```

This can include:

- your notes and their contents,
- note attachments you choose to save,
- app settings (colours, layout, startup preference),
- local encryption material used to protect the notes file on this device.

This data is written and read by the app on your computer. It is never transmitted anywhere by the app.

## 2. What data the app collects

**None.**

- **No personal data is collected.**
- **No telemetry or usage statistics** are collected.
- **No analytics, crash reporting, or diagnostic services** are used.
- **No third-party SDKs** for analytics, advertising, attribution, or tracking are included in the app.
- **No accounts, sign-in, or email collection** exist in the app.

## 3. Internet and network use

Rim Notes **operates completely offline**. The application does not require an internet connection and does not open network connections. Its only network-related capability on your device is the standard Windows mechanism that starts the app when you sign in — which sends no data anywhere.

## 4. Sharing and selling of data

Because no data is collected, **nothing is shared, sold, rented, or transferred** to third parties. The app contains no code that uploads your notes, settings, or any other content.

## 5. Payments

Rim Notes is a paid application. **The app itself never sees or handles your payment details** (card numbers, billing address, etc.). Payments are processed entirely by the store or reseller you buy it from (for example, the Microsoft Store), whose own privacy policy governs that transaction.

## 6. Where your data lives and how to delete it

- All notes and settings remain in `%AppData%\RimNotes\` on your device.
- They are not copied anywhere else by the app.
- To remove everything permanently, delete the `%AppData%\RimNotes\` folder (the app can also delete individual notes from within the app). Uninstalling the app does not always remove this folder automatically.

## 7. Children's privacy

Rim Notes does not collect any data from anyone, including children. Because the app is fully offline and account-free, it is suitable for use without any data-privacy concern for minors; parental supervision of *content* is, as with any notes app, up to the user.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour ever changes in a way that involves data handling, this policy will be updated before such behaviour ships, and the effective date above will be revised. The current version is always the one shipped with (or linked from) the app.
